# One-line speaker notes

SDS Career Services · Reggie Leonard

**01 · 0:00–1:00 · Orient**  
Today you will choose one piece of work and practice developing it, explaining it, and introducing it to someone else.

**02 · 1:00–2:00 · Orient**  
The work builds fluency; explaining what you did helps other people recognize your capability.

**03 · 2:00–5:00 · Exercise**  
Write three possibilities, then circle one that you will keep using throughout the session.

**04 · 5:00–9:00 · Watch**  
Look for a task in a job description, then reduce its scale until you can practice part of it with the access and experience you already have.

**05 · 9:00–12:00 · Try with us**  
Choose one small part of this task that a student could practice, and explain why your version is manageable.

**06 · 12:00–15:00 · Try on yours**  
Revisit the work you selected and identify the gap between what you can do now and what you would like to understand or practice.

**07 · 15:00–18:00 · Watch**  
A useful project explanation tells us the problem, your approach and contribution, and what happened without turning a plan into a result.

**08 · 18:00–21:00 · Try with us**  
Before rewriting an explanation, ask for the information that would let a reader understand the work.

**09 · 21:00–24:00 · Try on yours**  
Explain the same work you selected, using completed work where it exists and clear future tense where it does not.

**10 · 24:00–26:00 · Orient**  
A project README explains one piece of work; your profile can introduce you and point toward a selection of projects.

**11 · 26:00–29:00 · Model + apply**  
Give a person enough context to understand why you are sharing this project, rather than sending an unexplained portfolio link.

**12 · 29:00–36:00 · Independent work**  
Choose a step connected to your existing work and use this time to make a concrete improvement you can explain.

**13 · 36:00–39:00 · Peer test**  
Let the work speak first, then explain one decision without reading the workshop’s template.

**14 · 39:00–40:00 · Reflect**  
Before you leave, save what changed and name one next action you know how to take.
