# One-line speaker notes

SDS Career Services · Reggie Leonard

**01 · 0:00–1:00 · Introduce**  
Your portfolio gives people a way to understand your work beyond the skills listed on your résumé.

**02 · 1:00–3:00 · Discuss**  
Doing the work builds your understanding; explaining it helps other people recognize what you can contribute.

**03 · 3:00–6:00 · Exercise**  
Write a few possibilities, compare them at your table, and choose something you would like to explore today.

**04 · 6:00–8:00 · Discuss**  
Look for tasks in a posting, then consider a smaller way to practice one of them.

**05 · 8:00–9:00 · Discuss**  
Choose projects that add relevant evidence rather than repeating the same exercise with different data.

**06 · 9:00–11:00 · Demonstrate**  
A useful README explains the question, your contribution, and what the work actually establishes.

**07 · 11:00–13:00 · Demonstrate**  
Your profile introduces you, a project README explains a project, and a separate website is one optional way to bring them together.

**08 · 13:00–14:00 · Discuss**  
Choose a form of evidence that respects the permissions attached to the work.

**09 · 14:00–16:00 · Demonstrate**  
Choose one relevant project and explain why you are sharing it with this person or audience.

**10 · 16:00–38:00 · Work and questions**  
Choose the activity that would help you most, work in your own file or tool, and ask questions as they come up.

**11 · 38:00–40:00 · Questions and close**  
Before you leave, save the work and make sure you know where to return when you need examples or help.
