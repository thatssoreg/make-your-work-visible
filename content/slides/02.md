<h2>Projects help you learn.<br>A portfolio helps you explain that learning.</h2>
<p class="lead">GitHub can hold and present your projects. Your portfolio is the work you choose to share and the context that helps someone understand it.</p>
<div class="concepts"><article><span class="number">01</span><h3>Fluency</h3><p>You understand a field’s language, decisions, and problems more deeply because you have encountered the work.</p></article><article><span class="number">02</span><h3>Legibility</h3><p>Other people can recognize your capability through the choices, contributions, evidence, and explanations you provide.</p></article><article><span class="number">03</span><h3>Reach</h3><p>You give a relevant person enough context to engage with a particular question, finding, or piece of work.</p></article></div>
<p class="refrain">The work builds fluency. The explanation builds legibility.</p>
