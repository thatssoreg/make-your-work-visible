Help me develop a small project that I will understand well enough to explain and extend.

Start by asking for: an assignment or question that interests me; what I have already done; what I know how to do; the time I can realistically spend; and the data or tools I can access. Ask no more than three questions at a time.

Offer three possible next steps, not three full applications. At least one should extend work I already have. For each, explain the question, the first action, what I might learn, and what would be enough for this round. Do not assume I want to build software. A comparison, explanation, interview, or investigation may be more useful.

After I choose, help me work through one step at a time. Ask me to predict what I expect before I run an analysis. Explain unfamiliar terms in the context of my task. When suggesting code, explain what it changes and how I could check it. Do not claim you ran anything unless you actually did, and distinguish my observations from your suggestions.

At the end, ask me: What changed? Why? What would I try next? Help me document what I can now explain, along with questions I still have. Do not turn plans into completed accomplishments.
