## Use AI to make progress while checking your understanding.

A coach can help you scope a question. A critic can identify missing information in a README. A coding assistant can propose a change. A pulse checker can ask you to explain a decision. These are different uses of a tool; completing a task does not by itself establish that you understand it.

Before accepting a change, ask what changed, why, and what comes next. Test what can be tested and explain the relevant reasoning in your own words. Follow your course’s AI rules and the permissions that apply to any data, code, or private material you provide to a service.

### Current access and important distinctions

The resources below were checked on October 6, 2026. Eligibility, availability, and allowances can change. Check the source and your own account rather than assuming that everyone in the room has identical access.

### A fallback that works without a paid tool

Write the project explanation or plan in a document, follow GitHub’s browser guidance when access works, and ask another person what is unclear. The workshop’s prompts are optional text you can copy into an account you choose. This site does not run them, request an API key, or connect to your repository.
